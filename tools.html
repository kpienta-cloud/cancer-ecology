The instruments

Six tools, three pillars, one program.

Each tool sits inside a single pillar of the framework — Risk, Detection, or Treatment — and each is mechanistically connected to the next. Together they are how the program tests itself. The G-Function Tool is live and publicly accessible; the others have active manuscripts and open repositories.

In development Risk

ExposoGraph 2.0

A multi-carcinogen pathway flux engine. Tissue-specific bioactivation and detoxification networks (CYP450, UGT, GST, NAT, SULT families) with genotype modifiers. Returns a per-person, per-tissue cancer risk score that varies up to 47-fold across plausible genotype configurations.

Pillar: Risk Manuscript: PLOS Computational Biology (under review) Repository: github.com/kpienta-cloud/Exposograph

In development Risk

GeoToxGraph

An evolutionary contrast map of human vs. microbial detoxification. Quantifies pathway loss in the human lineage relative to extremophile bacteria — most notably Geobacter — and identifies the chemicals for which the loss is the cancer story.

Pillar: Risk Manuscript: in revision Repository: github.com/kpienta-cloud/geobacter

In development Treatment

Prairie-Dogs G-function Simulator

An interactive evolutionary game-theoretic model of tumor ecology. Adaptive dynamics of cell populations under therapy, resource competition, and engineered niches — anchored in the prairie-dog town homology and producing the G-function (fitness generating function) for the system. The scientific basis of adaptive therapy.

Pillar: Treatment Manuscript: perspective in revision Repository: github.com/kpienta-cloud/Prairie-Dogs

Live Treatment

G-Function Tool

An interactive web-based evolutionary game theory simulator for cancer adaptive dynamics. The first instrument in the Center to be publicly deployed. Maintained by the Cancer Adaptive Landscapes team — a cross-institutional collaboration spanning Hopkins, Princeton, Moffitt, and Lund.

Pillar: Treatment Status: Live at tool.cancerecology.com Team: Cancer Adaptive Landscapes

In development Treatment

CEDT — Cancer Ecology Digital Twin

A patient-level state-space ecological forecast with Bayesian data assimilation. State vector X(t) = [H, E, T, M, Q] couples host, exposome, tumor, therapy, and quality-of-life; a GLV core describes clone interactions; sparse clinical observations continuously update the posterior. The instrument that individualizes adaptive dosing.

Pillar: Treatment Manuscript: CEDT v6.5, in submission Repository: github.com/kpienta-cloud/CEDT

In development Detection

IsoMCED — Isotope Multi-Cancer Early Detection

A hair-keratin stable-isotope fingerprint — δ13C, δ15N, δ34S — as a non-invasive multi-cancer early-detection screen. A 5 cm strand carries a six-month metabolic record; under twenty dollars per sample at population scale.

Pillar: Detection (deployable diagnostic) Status: pilot funded · Biswas Fast Grant Partner: Kreftect, Inc. (CAP/CLIA pathway prep)

Cancer Adaptive Landscapes

The cross-institutional team behind the G-Function Tool.

A seven-investigator collaboration applying evolutionary game theory to cancer adaptive dynamics. The G-Function Tool is the team's first public instrument; further work spans landscape modeling, dormancy theory, and clinical translation.
